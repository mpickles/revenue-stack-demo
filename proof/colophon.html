How this site was made

The site is a piece of work too. These are the choices behind it.

Type

Aa
IBM Plex Sans, for everything you read. Weight 400 and 500 only.
gclid=Cj0KCQ 37/37
IBM Plex Mono, for data only: dates, statuses, tool names, numbers. Never for decoration.

Colour

Off-white and near-black, never pure. One accent colour, picked at random each visit. Press one to try it.

Motion

  • Things move once, when you reach them, and then stay put.
  • Nothing loops. The one background ripple ends in under five seconds.
  • The dot grid only moves where your pointer is.
  • With "reduce motion" on, nothing moves at all.

Small things

  • Borders are see-through ink, so they sit right on any background.
  • The nav corner is the button corner plus the gap around it.
  • Numbers in tables are all the same width.
  • Wide tables fade at the edge that still scrolls.

What I left out

  • Gradient blobs, glass cards and floating particles.
  • Numbered section labels and tiny all-caps headings.
  • A cursor that replaces yours.
  • Any number I cannot back up.
