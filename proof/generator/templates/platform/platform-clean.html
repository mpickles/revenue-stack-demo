What I chose, and what I turned down

Chosedbt for the models and tests.

Turned downDataform.

Why, and what it costA failed dbt test blocks everything after it by default. I am the only builder, so the tests are the safety net.

ChoseRebuild recent days only. Look back 35 days for late conversions. Full refresh on Sundays.

Turned downRebuilding all of history every morning.

Why, and what it costAbout 30 GB a day became about 7.5. The cost: 369 ad statuses changed when arbitrary picks became consistent.

ChoseSaved copies of the tested tables for every screen.

Turned downA live query per page, or a paid cache at about $30 a month per GiB.

Why, and what it costPages load fast and cost nothing. The cost: data is only as fresh as the last build.
