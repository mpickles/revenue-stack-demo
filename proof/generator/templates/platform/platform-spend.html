The two workflows

Search terms review

Rules drop what is already handled. The model judges the rest. Code re-checks every answer.

First run: 35 of 89 terms excluded. It never excludes a term that converted.

Budget pacing

Projects month-end spend and recommends a daily budget for what is left.

A budget can never be saved above its parent.

How each one decides

Search terms review

  1. 1

    Rules drop terms already handled: already a keyword, already blocked, or decided in the last 90 days.

  2. 2

    The model judges what is left.

  3. 3

    Code re-checks every answer, and sends brand and competitor terms to their own lists.

  4. 4

    A term that converted, one that would block a live keyword, or a campaign under 14 days old goes to a person as "Your call".

Budget pacing

  1. 1

    Projected spend is what is spent so far, plus the 7-day daily average times the days left.

  2. 2

    Recommended daily budget is what is left divided by the days left. Never below zero.

  3. 3

    A recommendation never moves a budget more than 20% in one step.

  4. 4

    Within 10% of plan counts as on track.

What I chose, and what I turned down

ChoseRules first, then the model, then code re-checks.

Turned downAsking the model to judge every term.

Why, and what it costMost terms need no judgement. The model only sees what the rules could not settle.

ChoseNegatives go on three shared lists only.

Turned downCampaign and ad group negatives.

Why, and what it costI checked the team's own sheets. All 121 of their excludes were on shared lists.

ChoseNo spend bar. An irrelevant term goes, whatever it spent.

Turned downA $10 minimum before a term could be excluded.

Why, and what it cost85 of those 121 excludes had spent under $10. My bar would have kept them all.

2 more choices

ChoseThe output is a sheet the lead pastes from.

Turned downWriting to the ad account, or an approve button in the app.

Why, and what it costClient money is on the line. Nothing writes until a write has its own test. The cost: a copy and paste.

ChosePacing uses fixed rules. No model.

Turned downAsking a model for the budget.

Why, and what it costIt is arithmetic. A model adds cost and a chance of being wrong.
