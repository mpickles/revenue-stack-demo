What it makes possible

Three tracks on one line: what changed, what the team did, and what the leads did next.

Why it is built the way it is

BecauseThe official route needs a developer token I did not have.
SoThe capture runs as a script inside the ad accounts.

No token needed.

BecauseThe official route silently drops rows past a cap.
SoThe script pages past the cap.

Nothing is lost on a busy account.

BecauseA change next to a result is not proof.
SoThe wording is "followed by", never "caused".

And leads are only quoted when both periods are complete.

What broke, and what I did about it

The first production build had zero rows.

Account IDs were written with dashes in one place and without in another.

FixOne format everywhere.
Changes got linked to the wrong task.

I reproduced it as four failing checks.

FixFixed. All 101 checks pass.
